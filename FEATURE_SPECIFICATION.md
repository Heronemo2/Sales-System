# Team Sales Management System — Feature Specification

## 1. Purpose and scope

The Team Sales Management System supports the Sales department's customer and order workflow: customer records, quotations, purchase orders, sales orders, deliveries, invoices, collections, returns, inventory visibility, and sales reporting.

This specification describes functionality visible in the current repository. It is not a claim that every workflow is complete or that every feature has been tested in production. The application is a PHP monolith using a Sales MySQL/MariaDB database and a separate Inventory System database.

## 2. User roles

| Role | Intended responsibilities | Current implementation notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sales Admin | Manage customers; review and approve sales records; monitor stockout status. | Admin pages enforce the Sales Admin role. |
| Sales Staff | Process quotations, purchase orders, orders, deliveries, billing, returns, and reports. | Sales workspace pages enforce the Sales Staff role. |
| Customer | Use the Customer-System portal after a Sales Admin approves the registration. | New registrations remain pending and cannot sign in until approved. |

## 4. Feature specifications

### 4.1 Sign-in, sign-out, and access control

- **Purpose:** Authenticate users and limit pages by role.
- **Actors:** Sales Admin and Sales Staff use this application; customers sign in to Customer-System after account approval.
- **Inputs:** Username, password, and acceptance of the Terms of Service.
- **Behavior:** Verify the password against its stored hash, create a PHP session, record the last-login time, and redirect to the sales dashboard. Sign-out ends the session.
- **Rules:** Unauthenticated users must sign in. Restricted pages check for their required role.
- **Acceptance criteria:**
  - Valid Sales Admin and Sales Staff credentials start a session.
  - Invalid credentials do not authenticate the user.
  - Users without the required role cannot access protected modules.
  - Sign-out clears the authenticated session.
- **Implementation/data:** [login.php](./login.php), [logout.php](./logout.php), [auth.php](./includes/auth.php), `users`, `activity_logs`.
- Customer registrations are created as pending. The Sales Admin must approve them before the Customer-System permits sign-in.
- **Implementation:** Customer account approval is handled in [customer_account_approvals.php](./customer_account_approvals.php); the `account_status` field is added by [20261107_add_customer_account_approval.sql](./database/migrations/20261107_add_customer_account_approval.sql).

### 4.2 Sales dashboard

- **Purpose:** Give staff and admins a high-level view of sales activity.
- **Actors:** Sales Admin and Sales Staff.
- **Behavior:** Display summary counts and amounts for pending quotations and customer requests, active orders, outstanding receivables, and stockout activity; show monthly sales invoiced, order count, average order value, collection rate, and recent order progress.
- **Rules:** Order progress is derived from the order, delivery, and invoice statuses. The shared completion helper treats an order as completed only when it is paid and delivered.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Summary cards, monthly KPIs, and recent orders reflect current Sales database records. Cancelled orders are excluded from monthly sales/order KPIs.
- **Implementation/data:** [dashboard.php](./dashboard.php), `customer_order_requests`, `sales_quotations`, `sales_orders`, `deliveries`, `sales_invoices`, `inventory_stockout_approvals`.

### 4.3 Customer management and transaction history

- **Purpose:** Maintain customer contact details and account status.
- **Actors:** Sales Admin.
- **Inputs:** Customer name, phone, email, address, and status.
- **Behavior:** Create and edit customers, activate/deactivate records, search the customer list, and view a customer's transaction timeline across quotations, purchase orders, sales orders, invoices, collections, and returns.
- **Rules:** Customer name is required; a supplied email must be valid; customer codes are generated by the application.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Admins can add and edit customer details, change status, and view the customer's linked transactions.
- **Implementation/data:** [admin_customers.php](./admin_customers.php), `customers`, related transaction tables.

### 4.4 Customer order-request tracking

- **Purpose:** Track requested products and quantities before a quotation is created.
- **Actors:** Sales Staff process requests; a customer is associated with each request.
- **Behavior:** Requests have a request number, customer, date, product code, quantity, notes, status, and optional linked quotation. The quotation workflow uses requests marked `Pending Quotation`, then updates them when a quotation is sent or converted.
- **Rules:** A request can be used to create a quotation while it is still pending and has not already been quoted.
- **Acceptance criteria:** A processed request is linked to its quotation and its status advances with the workflow.
- **Implementation/data:** Request handling is visible in [quotations.php](./quotations.php) and the dashboard; schema table `customer_order_requests`.
- **Known limitation:** The current PHP pages show how requests are consumed, but no dedicated request-submission page or endpoint was found. Request creation/customer self-service should be confirmed before treating it as an available feature.

### 4.5 Quotation management and stock checking

- **Purpose:** Prepare and track customer price quotations.
- **Actors:** Sales Staff; Sales Admin reviews quotations; a customer decision is represented in the workflow.
- **Inputs:** Source request, quotation date, validity date, product, and requested quantity. The fixed unit price comes from Team Sales inventory.
- **Behavior:** Create a quotation from a pending request, calculate the total from the inventory price, check stock/product details through the Inventory integration, save the Inventory check result, and track approval statuses. Quotations can be exported.
- **Rules:** A fixed inventory price must be greater than zero; Sales Staff cannot override it in a quotation; totals must be valid; a request cannot be quoted again once processed. Sales stock is considered when determining whether a quotation can proceed.
- **Acceptance criteria:** A valid request creates one numbered quotation using the fixed inventory price and links it to the customer/request; missing price or stock-check errors are reported without creating a quotation.
- **Implementation/data:** [quotations.php](./quotations.php), [quotation_export.php](./quotation_export.php), `sales_quotations`, `customer_order_requests`, Inventory System product/stock data.

### 4.6 Sales Admin approvals

- **Purpose:** Review and approve or reject sales workflow records requiring administrative review.
- **Actors:** Sales Admin.
- **Behavior:** Provide an administrative review surface for pending sales approvals, including quotation-related approval and other supported approval statuses.
- **Rules:** Approval actions are restricted to Sales Admin; status changes should be reflected in the originating sales record and activity history.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Admins can review pending items and update their approval status; non-admin users cannot perform admin approval actions.
- **Implementation/data:** [admin_approvals.php](./admin_approvals.php), `sales_quotations`, `sales_orders`, `sales_returns`, `activity_logs`.

### 4.7 Purchase orders

- **Purpose:** Track the Sales-side purchase-order document created after a quotation is accepted.
- **Actors:** Sales Staff.
- **Behavior:** List and search purchase orders; convert an open purchase order into a sales order after checking current Inventory product availability.
- **Rules:** A purchase order must be open and have enough product stock at conversion time. If the stock check fails or stock is insufficient, it remains open.
- **Acceptance criteria:** An eligible purchase order creates a linked sales order and becomes converted exactly once; an ineligible order remains open with a useful error.
- **Implementation/data:** [purchase_orders.php](./purchase_orders.php), [po_export.php](./po_export.php), `purchase_orders`, `sales_quotations`, `customers`, `sales_orders`.
- **Terminology:** This is the Sales workflow's document, not a raw-material procurement module.

### 4.8 Sales orders and approval

- **Purpose:** Manage customer orders created from purchase orders.
- **Actors:** Sales Staff manage and view orders; Sales Admin approves or rejects them.
- **Behavior:** Display and search orders, show approval/payment/delivery progress, and link to downstream delivery and invoicing.
- **Rules:** Orders originate from purchase orders. An approved order can proceed to delivery and invoice creation. Completion requires both full payment and delivery.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Each order retains its links to the source quotation/customer; its status and next action are visible; approval rules prevent unapproved orders from proceeding to restricted downstream actions.
- **Implementation/data:** [orders.php](./orders.php), [purchase_orders.php](./purchase_orders.php), `sales_orders`, `purchase_orders`, `sales_quotations`, `customers`.

### 4.9 Inventory lookup and stockout synchronization

- **Purpose:** Check product availability and reflect stock transferred from the Inventory System to Team Sales.
- **Actors:** Sales Staff and Sales Admin; the Inventory System owns product and stockout approval.
- **Behavior:** Use Inventory product details when evaluating quotation stock; synchronize Inventory stockout records; show Team Sales stock and stockout statuses.
- **Rules:** Inventory is the authoritative source for product master data and stockout approval. Sales does not approve an Inventory stockout. Team Sales stock is tracked separately and is added after an approved Inventory stockout is synchronized.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Quotation stock checks use current Inventory product details and Team Sales stock; a synchronized stockout is visible with its status and reference.
- **Implementation/data:** [inventory.php](./inventory.php), [inventory_stockout_approval.php](./inventory_stockout_approval.php), [functions.php](./includes/functions.php), [inventory_db.php](./config/inventory_db.php), `inventory`, `inventory_stockout_approvals`.

### 4.10 Delivery tracking

- **Purpose:** Track fulfillment and delivery status for an approved sales order.
- **Actors:** Sales Staff.
- **Behavior:** A delivery is created as part of order approval; staff can view/search deliveries and update status from Preparing to In Transit to Delivered.
- **Rules:** Inventory deduction is associated with the order-to-delivery process. A sales order is not complete until both paid and delivered.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Delivery status changes are stored and visible; reaching Delivered triggers order-completion synchronization where payment is already complete.
- **Implementation/data:** [delivery.php](./delivery.php), [functions.php](./includes/functions.php), `deliveries`, `sales_orders`, `inventory`.

### 4.11 Sales invoicing

- **Purpose:** Issue and view customer invoices for approved sales orders.
- **Actors:** Sales Staff.
- **Behavior:** Create an invoice for an approved order that does not already have one; list and display invoice details.
- **Rules:** At most one invoice is created through the page for a sales order. Initial paid amount/status reflect the order's payment status. Invoice creation updates the order's invoiced/completion state.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Only eligible orders appear for invoice creation; invoice total and customer/order references match the source order; duplicate invoicing is prevented.
- **Implementation/data:** [invoices.php](./invoices.php), `sales_invoices`, `sales_orders`, `customers`.

### 4.12 Billing, collections, receivables, and balances

- **Purpose:** Track invoice balances and customer payments.
- **Actors:** Sales Staff.
- **Behavior:** View billing receipts and open balances; record one or more collections against an invoice; show accounts receivable and customer balance summaries.
- **Inputs:** Invoice, collection date, due date, payment amount, and method (Cash, GCash, or Bank Transfer).
- **Rules:** Collection amount must be positive and cannot exceed the remaining invoice balance. Invoice status becomes Partially Paid or Paid based on the accumulated amount. Order completion is synchronized after payment.
- **Acceptance criteria:** A valid collection creates a collection record and updates invoice paid amount/status; invalid or excessive payments are rejected; balances reflect recorded collections.
- **Implementation/data:** [customer_account.php](./customer_account.php), [collection.php](./collection.php), [receivables.php](./receivables.php), [balance.php](./balance.php), `collections`, `sales_invoices`, `account_receivable`, `customer_balance`.

### 4.13 Sales returns

- **Purpose:** Record returns associated with completed or existing sales orders.
- **Actors:** Sales Staff create return records; Sales Admin approval is represented in return status.
- **Inputs:** Sales order, reason, return date, and optional return amount.
- **Behavior:** Generate a return number, store a pending return, and display/search return history and approval status.
- **Rules:** An order, reason, and date are required.
- **Acceptance criteria:** A valid submission is linked to its order and appears in the return list as pending review.
- **Implementation/data:** [returns.php](./returns.php), [admin_approvals.php](./admin_approvals.php), `sales_returns`, `sales_orders`.

### 4.14 Sales reports and exports

- **Purpose:** Summarize sales performance and customer transaction activity.
- **Actors:** Sales Staff.
- **Behavior:** Show annual and monthly invoice sales, year-over-year change, quotation-to-order conversion, average collection time, top customer performance, and selected-customer workflow history. Provide a formal report export.
- **Rules:** Report figures are derived from Sales database records and the selected year/customer filters.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Changing the year updates time-based totals and the export; customer selection shows that customer's linked transactions.
- **Implementation/data:** [reports.php](./reports.php), [export_sales_report.php](./export_sales_report.php), sales and billing tables.

### 4.15 User profile and account settings

- **Purpose:** Let an authenticated user maintain basic profile details and credentials.
- **Actors:** Authenticated users.
- **Behavior:** Update display name, username, and avatar; change password after verifying the current password; show account preference controls.
- **Rules:** Required profile fields cannot be blank; avatar formats and upload size are restricted; password change requires the correct current password and matching new-password confirmation.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Successful updates persist and are reflected in the current session; invalid inputs produce an error and do not report success.
- **Implementation/data:** [manage_profile.php](./manage_profile.php), [account_settings.php](./account_settings.php), `users`, `assets/uploads/avatars/`.
- **Note:** Preference controls are displayed in the current page; persistence of those preferences is not confirmed.

### 4.16 Help center

- **Purpose:** Provide in-application guidance for common workflows and support.
- **Actors:** Authenticated users.
- **Behavior:** Explain dashboard navigation, quotation-to-order workflow, inventory interaction, billing, and password changes.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Help content is accessible from the application layout.
- **Implementation:** [help_center.php](./help_center.php).

### 4.17 Document exports

- **Purpose:** Provide printable/downloadable sales documents.
- **Actors:** Authenticated Sales Admin and Sales Staff; customers can view and print only their own invoices in Customer-System.
- **Behavior:** Export quotation, purchase-order, invoice, payment-receipt, and delivery-receipt PDFs, plus the formal sales report. Sales invoice, payment-receipt, and delivery-receipt PDFs open inline in the browser so they can be reviewed and printed; customer invoices are scoped to the signed-in customer.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Exported documents use the selected record and display the corresponding customer and transaction information; invoice, receipt, and delivery PDFs are viewable before printing; customer invoice requests cannot access another customer's record.
- **Implementation:** [quotation_export.php](./quotation_export.php), [po_export.php](./po_export.php), [document_export.php](./document_export.php), [invoices.php](./invoices.php), [export_sales_report.php](./export_sales_report.php), [simple_pdf.php](./includes/simple_pdf.php).

## 5. Shared business rules and data

- The Sales database is `team_sales`; its schema is maintained in [Team_Sales_Schema.sql](./database/Team_Sales_Schema.sql).
- Transaction records are related through customer, quotation, purchase-order, sales-order, delivery, invoice, collection, and return identifiers.
- Human-readable document numbers are generated for requests, quotations, purchase orders, orders, deliveries, invoices, collections, and returns.
- Inventory product IDs and product codes are cross-system references; Inventory is a separate database and is not constrained by Sales database foreign keys.
- Activity events are stored in `activity_logs` for supported actions.
- Sales-order completion is based on both delivery and payment state.

## 6. Out of scope or not confirmed

- Inventory procurement, manufacturing, and production planning are outside this Sales application.
- Applying the account approval migration is required before deploying the updated Customer-System registration and login flow.
- Customer order-request submission is not confirmed in the current PHP pages; existing request records are consumed by quotation processing.
- Durable persistence of the account preference toggles is not confirmed.
